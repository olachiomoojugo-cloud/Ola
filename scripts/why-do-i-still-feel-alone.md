# "Texting God: Why Do I Still Feel Alone?"

**Episode 2** of the Texting God series
**Format:** Animated chat between a young woman (the narrator) and God
**Narrator:** The same young woman from Episode 1. She reads her own messages *and* reads God's replies out loud as they arrive.
**Topic:** Loneliness: why we feel alone even around people, and practical ways out
**Runtime:** about 9 minutes (about 950 spoken words, plus typing pauses and silences)

---

## HOW TO READ THIS SCRIPT

Same tags as Episode 1:

| Tag | What it means |
|---|---|
| **ME:** | A message she types. Natural, honest, a little raw. |
| **GOD:** | A message from God. Read slower and softer, about 20% slower. |
| *(VO)* | Speaking straight to the viewer. |
| `[SCREEN]` | What the animation shows. |
| `[SFX]` / `[MUSIC]` | Sound cues. |
| ⏸ | A beat of silence. **Keep it.** |

**Chat look:** identical to Episode 1 (dark mode, gold "Father" bubbles, "Always online", typing dots), with two changes:
- The clock reads **11:58 PM, Friday**, not 2:47 AM.
- The step counter is now a small gold **heart that fills up**, one segment at a time: **1 / 7 … 7 / 7**.

## 0:00 – COLD OPEN (THE HOOK)

`[SCREEN]` A phone in the dark. **11:58 PM, Friday.** She's scrolling Instagram stories: blurred photos of a party, a birthday dinner, a group selfie. Everyone she knows is in them. She isn't.
`[SFX]` Faint muffled party music from someone's story. Then silence.

`[SCREEN]` She closes the app and opens the chat with "Father."

**ME:** Can I ask You something without You giving me a Sunday school answer?

`[SCREEN]` "• • •"

**GOD:** Ask Me the real one.

**ME:** Why do I still feel alone?

**ME:** I have people. I go to church. I have 600 followers. And I still feel like nobody actually knows me.

⏸
`[SCREEN]` "• • •" holds for 3 seconds.

**GOD:** Because being surrounded and being known are not the same thing.

**GOD:** And I'm going to tell you something about loneliness that nobody told you.

`[MUSIC]` A soft piano starts.
`[SCREEN]` Title slams in: **"I ASKED GOD WHY I STILL FEEL ALONE"**

*(VO)* If you've ever sat in a full room and felt invisible, this one's for you. What He told me in this chat, especially the last thing, I've never heard a pastor say. And it made me cry.

---

## 0:45 – THE PROBLEM

**ME:** Every Friday it's the same. I tell myself I'm fine. I'm "just introverted." I'm "focusing on me." But at night it gets loud.

**GOD:** I know. I'm up at night too.

**ME:** 😅 Okay but seriously. Is something wrong with me?

**GOD:** No. Loneliness is not a sign that something is wrong with you.

**GOD:** It's a signal. Like hunger. Hunger doesn't mean you're broken. It means you were made to be fed.

`[TRUTH CARD]` **"Loneliness isn't a flaw. It's a signal that you were made for connection."**

**GOD:** I said it Myself, at the very beginning: "It is not good for man to be alone." (Genesis 2:18)

**GOD:** I put that ache in you on purpose. Now let Me show you what to do with it.

`[SCREEN]` The gold heart appears in the corner, empty. **1 / 7**

---

## 1:40 – TRUTH 1: ALONE IS NOT ABANDONED

**ME:** But it really feels like You left too.

**GOD:** Feelings are real. But they're not always right.

**GOD:** I will never leave you nor forsake you. (Hebrews 13:5)

**GOD:** You can feel alone and still not be alone. Both can be true at 11:58 on a Friday night.

`[TRUTH CARD]` **"You can feel alone and still not be alone."**

`[TODAY'S ASSIGNMENT]` 📝 *When the loneliness gets loud tonight, say it out loud: "I feel alone, but I am not abandoned."*

---

## 2:20 – TRUTH 2: SOMETIMES YOU'RE JUST EXHAUSTED

`[SCREEN]` Heart: **2 / 7**

**GOD:** When did you last sleep properly?

**ME:** …define properly.

**GOD:** That's what I thought.

**GOD:** Elijah once sat under a tree and told Me he wanted to give up. He said, "I'm the only one left." (1 Kings 19:4, 10)

**GOD:** Do you know what I did first? I didn't preach at him. I let him sleep. Then I sent an angel with bread. Twice.

**ME:** Wait. You fed him before You fixed him?

**GOD:** Exactly. A tired body tells lonely stories.

`[TRUTH CARD]` **"A tired body tells lonely stories."**

`[TODAY'S ASSIGNMENT]` 📝 *Before you decide nobody cares: eat a real meal, drink water, and sleep 8 hours. Then see how loud the loneliness is.*

---

## 3:20 – TRUTH 3: YOU'RE SEEN, BUT NOT KNOWN

`[SCREEN]` Heart: **3 / 7**

**GOD:** When someone asks how you are, what do you say?

**ME:** "I'm good! You?" 🙂

**GOD:** And is that true?

⏸

**ME:** …not usually.

**GOD:** Then here's the hard truth. People can't love the version of you that you keep hiding.

**GOD:** You built a wall to stay safe. But the same wall that keeps pain out keeps love out too.

**GOD:** Carry each other's burdens. (Galatians 6:2) They can't carry what you never hand them.

`[TRUTH CARD]` **"The wall that keeps pain out keeps love out too."**

`[TODAY'S ASSIGNMENT]` 📝 *The next time someone you trust asks "How are you?", tell them one honest sentence.*

---

## 4:20 – TRUTH 4: SOMEBODY HAS TO GO FIRST

`[SCREEN]` Heart: **4 / 7**

**ME:** But I always reach out first. Nobody ever texts me first.

**GOD:** Can I show you something?

`[SCREEN]` Her recent texts scroll past, blurred. Most of them end with *her* not replying.

**ME:** …oh.

**GOD:** You're not as forgotten as you think. Some of them reached out. You just didn't answer, because you were tired, or scared, or busy feeling alone.

**GOD:** A person who wants friends must show themselves friendly. (Proverbs 18:24, NKJV)

**GOD:** Go first. Not because they deserve it. Because that's what I did for you.

`[TRUTH CARD]` **"Friendship starts with someone brave enough to go first."**

`[TODAY'S ASSIGNMENT]` 📝 *Text 3 people tonight. Not "hey." Something real: "I was thinking about you. Coffee this week?"*

---

## 5:20 – PATTERN BREAK (RETENTION RESET)

`[SCREEN]` A notification drops down: *"Group chat 'Sunday Squad': Tolu: Who's coming to brunch tomorrow? 🥞"*
`[SFX]` Buzz.

**ME:** Wait. I didn't even know I was still in this group chat.

**GOD:** You muted it in March.

**ME:** …how do You know that?

**GOD:** 😄 I know everything. Reply to Tolu.

`[SCREEN]` She types: *"Me! What time?"* Sends it. Three hearts pop up from the group instantly. ❤️❤️❤️

`[SFX]` Three soft *pings*.

---

## 5:50 – TRUTH 5: CONNECTION IS BUILT, NOT FOUND

`[SCREEN]` Heart: **5 / 7**

**ME:** Okay that was kind of beautiful. But one brunch won't fix this.

**GOD:** You're right. One brunch won't. Fifty brunches will.

**GOD:** You keep looking for a best friend like you'll find them already made. Real friendship is built slowly, by showing up to the same place, with the same people, again and again.

**GOD:** Don't give up meeting together, as some are in the habit of doing. (Hebrews 10:25)

`[TRUTH CARD]` **"Community isn't found. It's built, one ordinary week at a time."**

`[TODAY'S ASSIGNMENT]` 📝 *Pick ONE place to show up every week for 3 months: a small group, a choir, a gym class, a volunteer team. Same day, same people.*

---

## 6:50 – TRUTH 6: SERVE SOMEONE LONELIER THAN YOU

`[SCREEN]` Heart: **6 / 7**

**GOD:** Do you know who else is awake right now feeling exactly like you?

**ME:** Who?

**GOD:** The new girl at church who sat alone last Sunday. Your neighbour who lost her husband in the summer. Your cousin who moved cities.

**ME:** I didn't even think about them.

**GOD:** Loneliness turns all your attention inward. Love turns it back out.

**GOD:** If you pour yourself out for others, your light will rise in the darkness. (Isaiah 58:10)

**GOD:** I set the lonely in families. (Psalm 68:6) Sometimes I do that by sending *you* to someone else's door.

`[TRUTH CARD]` **"The cure for your loneliness might be someone else's."**

`[TODAY'S ASSIGNMENT]` 📝 *Find the person sitting alone this Sunday and sit next to them.*

---

## 7:50 – TRUTH 7: THE TWIST (EMOTIONAL PEAK)

`[SCREEN]` Heart: **7 / 7**. It glows.
`[MUSIC]` Cuts out completely.

**ME:** Can I ask one more thing?

**GOD:** Always.

**ME:** Do You even know what this feels like? You're God. You've never been lonely.

`[SCREEN]` "• • •" … holds … holds … holds for 4 seconds.

**GOD:** The night before I died, I asked My three closest friends to stay awake with Me while I prayed.

**GOD:** They fell asleep. Three times. (Matthew 26:40)

⏸

**GOD:** The next day, on the cross, I cried out, "My God, My God, why have You forsaken Me?" (Matthew 27:46)

**GOD:** I know loneliness better than anyone who has ever lived.

`[MUSIC]` The piano comes back in, slow and full.

**GOD:** I was forsaken so that you would never have to be.

`[TRUTH CARD]` **"He was forsaken, so you never would be."**

`[SCREEN]` Her typing indicator appears… disappears… appears.

**ME:** I don't know what to say.

**GOD:** You don't have to say anything. Just stop hiding from Me too.

## 8:50 – THE CLOSE

**ME:** Okay. I'm going to bed. Brunch tomorrow. 🥞

**GOD:** Good. And one more thing.

**ME:** ?

**GOD:** You were never as alone as you felt tonight.

**GOD:** I was here the whole time. I'll be at brunch too.

**ME:** 😂 You're coming to brunch?

**GOD:** I go everywhere you go. (Joshua 1:9)

`[SFX]` Soft *ding*. "Seen." The clock flips to **12:00 AM. Saturday.** The phone dims to black.

*(VO)* If you felt seen in this video, I want you to do something right now. Scroll down and comment "I'm not alone." You'll see you're really not.

*(VO)* And then text one person. Today. Not tomorrow.

*(VO)* Subscribe for the next chat, because next time I'm asking Him the question that keeps me up the most.

## PRODUCTION NOTES

### What's new in this episode
- **The clock.** 11:58 PM on a Friday, flipping to 12:00 AM Saturday at the end. Viewers who saw Episode 1 will notice the change of time, and the flip marks "a new day."
- **The filling heart** replaces the 1/7 counter. Same function, but it builds the emotional theme visually.
- **The group-chat moment (5:20)** is the episode's "win": three hearts pop up the moment she replies. Make it warm and quick. It's the most clippable 20 seconds in the video.

### Delivery
- The twist (7:50) is the heart of the episode. Read the Gethsemane lines **slowly**, almost in a whisper. Don't fill the silence after "They fell asleep. Three times."
- Keep the humour light and real: "…define properly," "You muted it in March," "You're coming to brunch?" The laughter is what makes the heavy moments land.

### Why this keeps people watching
1. **Instant relatability.** Everyone has watched stories of a party they weren't invited to.
2. **Two open loops:** "something about loneliness nobody told you" and "the last thing made me cry."
3. **The filling heart** shows progress through all 7 truths.
4. **A pattern break with a payoff** at 5:20 (the group chat).
5. **The emotional twist:** Jesus Himself was lonely, a fresh angle most viewers won't expect.
6. **A comment prompt that builds community:** "Comment 'I'm not alone'" fills the comments with people who feel the same, which helps the video's reach.

### Shorts (7 ready-made clips)
- "Is something wrong with me?" → "Loneliness is a signal, like hunger." (0:45)
- "You fed him before You fixed him?" (Truth 2)
- "The wall that keeps pain out keeps love out too." (Truth 3)
- "You muted it in March." (Pattern break, the funniest clip)
- "Do You even know what this feels like?" → Gethsemane (the twist, the strongest clip)
- "You're coming to brunch?" "I go everywhere you go." (Close)
