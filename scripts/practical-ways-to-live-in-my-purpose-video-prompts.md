# Video Prompts: "Texting God: How Do I Actually Live My Purpose?"

Shot-by-shot prompts for AI video tools (Runway, Kling, Veo, Sora, Pika, Hailuo, ManyMotions and similar), matched to the timestamps in `practical-ways-to-live-in-my-purpose.md`.

---

## READ THIS FIRST: HOW TO BUILD THE VIDEO

The video is made of **two layers**. Make them separately, then put them together in your editor (CapCut, Premiere, DaVinci, After Effects).

| Layer | What it is | How to make it |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Chat screen** | The phone UI: bubbles, typing dots, timestamps, counter, truth cards, sticky notes | **Make this in your editor, not with AI.** AI video tools still garble written text. Use a chat template (CapCut has several "fake text message" templates) or a chat-story maker, then style it with the specs in Section 2. |
| **B. Cinematic shots** | The narrator in their room, hands on the phone, symbolic visuals behind each step | **Make these with AI** using the prompts in Section 3. Use them as full-screen cutaways, or blurred behind the chat. |

**Editing pattern that holds attention:** chat screen (5–15 s) → cinematic shot (2–4 s) → chat screen → … Never stay on one layer longer than about 15 seconds.

**Golden rules for every AI prompt:**
1. **Never show God as a person.** God is always light: a warm glow, sunrise, a lamp, rays through a window. This keeps it reverent and stops the AI inventing a face.
2. **Never ask the AI for readable text** on screens, notes or signs. Ask for a "glowing phone screen" and lay your own text over it.
3. **Paste the same STYLE and CHARACTER blocks into every prompt.** That is how you keep the same person and look across 30 clips.
4. **Generate 5-second clips.** You can slow them down or loop them in the editor. Short clips come out cleaner.
5. **Pick one aspect ratio** and stick with it: **16:9** for the main video, **9:16** for Shorts. Most prompts below work for both.

## SECTION 1: REUSABLE BLOCKS (paste these into every prompt)

### STYLE block
> Cinematic, moody, intimate. Dark bedroom at night, deep navy and charcoal shadows, warm gold light as the only accent colour. Shallow depth of field, soft film grain, gentle anamorphic lens flares, slow, deliberate camera movement. Photoreal, 35mm film look, high detail, emotional, quiet.

*(Want a cartoon or 3D look instead of photoreal? Swap the last sentence for: "Stylised 3D animation, soft Pixar-like lighting, smooth shading, expressive but subtle faces." Use the same version for every clip.)*

### CHARACTER block: pick ONE and use it every time

**Option A, young man:**
> A young Black man around 24, short tapered haircut, light beard, warm brown skin, wearing an oversized faded grey hoodie. Tired but thoughtful eyes.

**Option B, young woman:**
> A young Black woman around 23, natural shoulder-length coily hair loosely tied up, warm brown skin, wearing an oversized faded grey hoodie. Tired but thoughtful eyes.

*(Change age, skin, hair or clothes to match you, but write it exactly the same way in every prompt. For even better consistency, generate one good portrait first and use it as an image reference in image-to-video.)*

### NEGATIVE prompt (for tools that support it)
> text, letters, words, captions, subtitles, watermark, logo, distorted hands, extra fingers, deformed face, face of God, bearded old man in clouds, cartoonish halo, oversaturated, harsh lighting, fast cuts, shaky camera

In the prompts below, `[STYLE]` and `[CHARACTER]` mean "paste the block here."

## SECTION 2: CHAT SCREEN SPECS (build in your editor)

| Element | Spec |
|---|---|
| Background | Dark mode `#0E0F13`. Faint blurred bedroom footage behind it at 15% opacity (use shot B-02 or B-03). |
| Status bar | Time **2:47 AM**. Battery at 12% (small realism touch). |
| Contact header | Name **"Father"**, status *"Always online"*. Avatar is a soft gold glowing circle, not a face. |
| ME bubbles | Right side, `#2A2C33` grey, white text, rounded 18 px. |
| GOD bubbles | Left side, warm gold gradient `#E8B04A` → `#C98A2B`, dark text `#1A1408`, soft outer glow. Fade in over 0.3 s with a slight scale-up (95% → 100%). |
| Typing dots | "• • •" in gold, pulsing. Normal: 1 s. Big lines: 2–3 s. |
| Counter | Top right, **1 / 7**, small gold pill. Flash on change. |
| Truth cards | The bubble's text lifts out and fills the screen. Bold serif (Playfair Display or Cormorant), cream on near-black, slow push-in, 2–3 s. |
| Assignment stickies | Yellow sticky note with a slight tilt, slides in from the bottom right, handwritten font (Caveat or Kalam), 📝 icon. |
| Sounds | Send = soft *whoosh*. God's reply = warm low *ding*. Typing = very faint tapping. |

## SECTION 3: SHOT-BY-SHOT AI PROMPTS

Each shot lists **when** it goes, **what it's for**, and the **prompt**.

### 0:00 – COLD OPEN

**B-01: Phone wakes in the dark** *(0:00, first frame, 3 s)*
> Extreme close-up of a smartphone lying face-up on a rumpled bed sheet in a pitch-black bedroom. The screen suddenly lights up, casting a soft cool-white glow across the fabric. Slow push-in. Dust particles float in the light. No readable text on the screen. [STYLE]

**B-02: Narrator awake in bed** *(0:03, 4 s)*
> [CHARACTER] lies awake in bed at night, staring at the ceiling, face lit only by the glow of a phone held at their chest. Slow overhead shot drifting down. Restless, heavy silence. The bedroom is dark except for the phone light. [STYLE]

**B-03: Thumbs typing** *(0:06, 4 s, loop it under the first messages)*
> Close-up of two thumbs hesitating over a glowing phone keyboard in a dark room, typing slowly, pausing, deleting, typing again. Shallow depth of field, the screen is blurred and unreadable. Warm skin tones lit by cool screen light. [STYLE]

**B-04: The first golden glow** *(0:12, right when God's first reply arrives, 3 s)*
> Close-up of [CHARACTER]'s face lit by a phone. The light on their face slowly shifts from cool white to a warm golden glow, as if something warm just appeared on the screen. Their eyes widen slightly and their breath catches. Very slow push-in. [STYLE]

**B-05: Title backdrop** *(0:35, behind the title card, 4 s)*
> Wide shot of a dark bedroom window at night. A thin beam of warm golden light slowly cuts across the room from the window, lighting floating dust particles. Slow dolly-in. Empty space in the centre for a title. [STYLE]

### 0:45 – THE PROBLEM

**B-06: Scrolling everyone else's life** *(0:45, 4 s)*
> Close-up of [CHARACTER] in bed scrolling a phone quickly with one thumb. The blurred, colourful light of other people's highlight reels (beaches, stages, celebrations) flickers across their tired face. Their expression slowly sinks. Unreadable screen. [STYLE]

**B-07: Same room, same questions** *(0:55, 4 s)*
> Wide, static shot of a small, slightly messy bedroom at night: laptop on a desk, work lanyard on a chair, clothes on the floor. [CHARACTER] sits on the edge of the bed, elbows on knees, phone in hand. Lonely, still. A clock on the wall, its face unreadable. [STYLE]

**B-08: Compass, not a map** *(1:05, under "Purpose isn't a destination. It's a direction.", 4 s)*
> A vintage brass compass on a dark wooden surface. Its needle spins, then slowly settles and points forward as a warm golden glow rises around it. Macro shot, slow orbit. [STYLE]

### 1:30 – STEP 1: IDENTITY BEFORE ASSIGNMENT

**B-09: Mirror moment** *(1:35, 4 s)*
> [CHARACTER] stands in front of a bathroom mirror at night, lit by a single warm bulb, looking at their own reflection with uncertainty. Slow push-in over the shoulder toward the reflection. Quiet, searching mood. [STYLE]

**B-10: Fingerprint of light** *(1:55, under Jeremiah 1:5 / Ephesians 2:10, 4 s)*
> Macro shot of a human fingertip. Its fingerprint ridges slowly fill with glowing golden light, like lines being drawn by hand. Dark background, floating golden particles. Symbolising being uniquely made. [STYLE]

**B-11: Name tags falling away** *(2:10, under "Assignment flows from identity", 4 s)*
> Slow-motion shot of plain blank name badges, a work lanyard and a student ID card falling away in the dark, tumbling softly, while a steady warm golden light glows behind them. No readable text on the cards. [STYLE]

### 2:30 – STEP 2: WHAT'S IN YOUR HAND

**B-12: Open hands** *(2:35, under "What is that in your hand?", 4 s)*
> Top-down close-up of [CHARACTER]'s open hands, palms up, resting on their lap, holding a smartphone. Warm golden light slowly pools in their palms. Slow push-in. Reverent, humble. [STYLE]

**B-13: Small faithful moments montage** *(2:50, three 3 s clips)*
> (a) [CHARACTER] at a kitchen counter in morning light, carefully helping a younger sibling with homework, both smiling. [STYLE]
> (b) [CHARACTER] at an office desk, leaning over to talk kindly with a quiet coworker sitting alone. Soft daylight. [STYLE]
> (c) [CHARACTER] alone at a desk late at night, focused, sketching or editing on a laptop, a warm lamp beside them. [STYLE]

**B-14: Hill before the mountain** *(3:15, under "You keep asking for the mountain…", 4 s)*
> Wide cinematic shot of a lone small figure walking up a gentle grassy hill at dawn. A huge mountain glows gold far in the distance behind it. Slow aerial drone push. [STYLE]

### 3:30 – STEP 3: FOLLOW WHAT BREAKS YOUR HEART

**B-15: Younger self** *(3:45, under "Kids who grow up thinking they're worthless", 4 s, play it quietly)*
> A small child around 9 sits alone on the steps of a school building at dusk, backpack beside them, head down, other children blurred and laughing in the background. Muted colours, soft melancholy, slow push-in. [STYLE]

**B-16: Tear in the dark** *(4:00, under "I know. I was there.", 3 s)*
> Extreme close-up of [CHARACTER]'s eye in the dark, glistening, a single tear forming and rolling down their cheek, lit by soft golden phone light. Barely any movement. Intimate. [STYLE]

**B-17: Rebuilding the wall** *(4:10, under Nehemiah 1:4, 4 s)*
> Close-up of hands placing an old weathered stone back into a broken ancient wall at sunrise, golden light breaking over the stones. Dust drifts in the light. Slow, hopeful. [STYLE]

### 4:30 – STEP 4: USE YOUR GIFT

**B-18: Buried talent** *(4:40, under the Matthew 25 line, 4 s)*
> Close-up of hands digging into dark soil. A small glowing gold coin is half-buried in the earth, its light fading as more soil covers it. Slow push-in. Moody, cautionary. [STYLE]

**B-19: The gift unburied** *(5:00, under "Comparison buries talents", 4 s)*
> Reverse of the last shot: hands brushing soil away from a gold coin that brightens and glows more and more as it is uncovered, light spilling upward. Slow motion, hopeful. [STYLE]

**B-20: Doing the gift** *(5:10, 4 s; pick the gift that fits your channel)*
> [CHARACTER] in a small, warmly lit room, confidently doing their gift (singing into a microphone / painting a canvas / filming with a camera / teaching a small group), a gentle smile, golden backlight. Slow arc around them. [STYLE]

### 5:20 – PATTERN BREAK

**B-21: The notification** *(5:20, 3 s)*
> Close-up of a phone in a dark room. A bright notification banner suddenly drops down at the top of the screen and the phone buzzes in the hand. A thumb moves toward it… and stops mid-air, hovering. Unreadable screen. [STYLE]

### 5:45 – STEP 5: FIRST, NOT LEFTOVERS

**B-22: Phone first thing** *(5:50, 3 s)*
> Early morning, a dim bedroom. A hand reaches out from under the covers and grabs a phone off the nightstand, the bright screen glaring in the dark. Slightly comedic, relatable. [STYLE]

**B-23: Before dawn, quiet place** *(6:10, under Mark 1:35, 4 s)*
> A lone figure sits on a rock on a quiet hillside before dawn, wrapped in a cloak, silhouetted against a deep blue sky that is just starting to turn gold at the horizon. Still, prayerful, peaceful. Very slow push-in. [STYLE]

**B-24: The new morning routine** *(6:25, 5 s)*
> Soft early-morning light through a window. [CHARACTER] sits on the floor beside their bed with an open Bible on their lap, phone face-down beside them, a cup of tea steaming. They close their eyes and breathe. Warm, peaceful. Slow push-in. [STYLE]

### 6:40 – STEP 6: GUARD YOUR CIRCLE

**B-25: Five silhouettes** *(6:45, 4 s)*
> Five blurred human silhouettes stand in a loose circle around [CHARACTER] in a dark, misty space. Four are lit with soft warm light; one stands slightly apart in cold red shadow. Slow overhead rotation. [STYLE]

**B-26: Walking with the wise** *(7:05, under Proverbs 13:20, 4 s)*
> [CHARACTER] walks side by side with an older mentor along a city sidewalk at golden hour, talking and laughing. The mentor gestures as if explaining something. Tracking shot from the front, warm lens flare. [STYLE]

### 7:30 – STEP 7: THE NEXT STEP

**B-27: Lamp, not floodlight** *(7:45, under "I don't give floodlights. I give lamps.", 5 s)*
> A dark stone path at night. A warm oil lamp held low in a hand lights only the single step directly in front of the feet. The path ahead is in complete darkness. Low-angle close shot, slow forward movement. [STYLE]

**B-28: Each step lights up** *(8:00, under "When you take it, I light the next one", 5 s)*
> Low-angle shot of bare feet stepping forward onto a dark path. With each step, the next stone ahead softly lights up with warm golden light, one at a time. Slow motion, magical but grounded. [STYLE]

**B-29: Sent** *(8:10, under "You'll feel sent", 4 s)*
> [CHARACTER] stands at a front door in the early morning, hand on the doorknob, takes a deep breath and opens it. Bright golden sunlight floods in over them. Shot from behind, slow push-in. [STYLE]

### 8:20 – THE TWIST

**B-30: Clock at 2:47** *(8:25, 3 s; or build it in your editor if the AI garbles the numbers)*
> Close-up of a simple analog wall clock in a dark bedroom, the second hand ticking slowly. Moonlight across the clock face. Quiet, heavy atmosphere. [STYLE]

**B-31: Locust field to harvest** *(8:40, under Joel 2:25, 5 s)*
> Time-lapse of a barren, dry, cracked field that slowly bursts back to life: green shoots rise and grow into a golden wheat field swaying in sunrise light. Wide shot, slow push-in. Restoration, hope. [STYLE]

**B-32: Relief** *(8:50, under "You didn't miss it…", 4 s)*
> [CHARACTER] sitting on their bed, phone in their lap, exhales and smiles through tears as the first light of dawn starts to fill the window behind them. Slow push-in. Release, peace. [STYLE]

### 9:00 – THE CLOSE

**B-33: Phone set down** *(9:05, under "Put the phone down.", 4 s)*
> Close-up of a hand gently placing a glowing phone face-down on a nightstand. As it touches the wood, the screen's glow fades out, and warm sunrise light from the window takes over the shot. [STYLE]

**B-34: "I'm coming with you"** *(9:15, 5 s; the final emotional shot)*
> [CHARACTER] walks out of their apartment building into the early morning street. A soft beam of golden sunlight seems to follow them, resting on their shoulders as they walk. Shot from behind, slow tracking, wide. Peaceful, hopeful, not alone. [STYLE]

**B-35: End-card backdrop** *(9:25, 6 s)*
> Wide shot of a city skyline at sunrise from a rooftop, golden light spreading across buildings, soft clouds, slow drift. Empty space in the frame for end-screen elements. [STYLE]
