# "Texting God: Why Is My Life So Heavy?"

**Episode 4** of the Texting God series
**Format:** Animated chat between a young woman (the narrator) and God
**Narrator:** The same young woman from Episodes 1–3. She reads her own messages *and* reads God's replies out loud as they arrive.
**Topic:** Burnout, overthinking and carrying too much, through Matthew 11:28–30 (*"My yoke is easy and My burden is light"*) and Matthew 6:33 (*"Seek first the kingdom of God"*)
**Tone:** Reflective and tender, with a few light moments of warmth. Quieter than Episodes 1 and 2, gentler than Episode 3.
**Runtime:** about 9 minutes (about 800 spoken words, plus pauses and on-screen moments)

---

## HOW TO READ THIS SCRIPT

| Tag | What it means |
|---|---|
| **ME:** | A message she types. Tired, honest, a little frayed. |
| **GOD:** | A message from God. Slow, gentle, unhurried, the calmest voice in the room. |
| *(VO)* | Speaking straight to the viewer. |
| `[SCREEN]` | What the animation shows. |
| `[SFX]` / `[MUSIC]` | Sound cues. |
| ⏸ | A beat of silence. **Keep it.** |

**Chat look:** the same dark mode and gold "Father" bubbles, with:
- The clock reads **Wednesday, 1:17 AM.**
- **The backpack.** In the corner of the screen sits a small illustrated backpack, overflowing with labelled stones. Through the episode, stones are taken out one by one, each with a soft *thunk* as it's set down. By the end, the bag is almost empty. This is the episode's visual thread.

**The stones in the bag** (each has a short label):
`Job` · `Side hustle` · `Serving at church` · `Being the strong friend` · `Everyone's yes` · `What if I fail?` · `5-year plan` · `Being behind` · `Making everyone proud` · `Tomorrow`

## 0:00 – COLD OPEN (THE HOOK)

`[SCREEN]` A laptop screen in a dark room: a to-do list. It scrolls… and scrolls. **23 items.** Three are ticked. The clock: **Wednesday, 1:17 AM.**
`[SFX]` A laptop fan humming. A long, tired exhale.

`[SCREEN]` She picks up her phone and opens the chat with "Father."

**ME:** I'm so tired.

**ME:** Not sleepy. Tired. In my bones.

**ME:** I'm doing everything right. I go to church. I serve. I work hard. I pray. So why do I feel like I'm drowning?

⏸
`[SCREEN]` "• • •" holds for 3 seconds.

**GOD:** Because you're carrying things I never handed you.

`[SCREEN]` The backpack appears in the corner, so full it's bulging, stones spilling over the top.

`[MUSIC]` A soft, slow piano begins.
`[SCREEN]` Title fades in: **"I ASKED GOD WHY MY LIFE FEELS SO HEAVY"**

*(VO)* If you're exhausted from doing everything "right", this one's for me as much as you. He told me something about one verse I'd read a hundred times, and I realised I'd never understood it. It's at the end. Don't skip it.

## 0:50 – "WHAT'S IN THE BAG?"

**GOD:** Tell Me what you're carrying.

**ME:** My job. My side hustle. Serving at church. Being there for everyone.

**ME:** Trying not to fall behind. Trying to make my parents proud. Figuring out the next five years.

**ME:** And every night, my brain plays every "what if" on repeat.

`[SCREEN]` As she lists them, labelled stones glow one by one inside the backpack.

**GOD:** Now look at the bag. Which of these did I give you?

**ME:** …all of them?

**GOD:** Look again.

⏸

`[TRUTH CARD]` **"You're tired because you're carrying things God never handed you."**

---

## 1:50 – THE VERSE ON THE WALL

**ME:** I know what You're going to say. "Come to Me, all who are weary and burdened, and I will give you rest."

**ME:** It's literally on my bedroom wall. It doesn't work.

**GOD:** Can I ask you something gently?

**GOD:** Have you ever actually come? Or have you just read the sign?

⏸

**ME:** …I read the sign.

**GOD:** Rest isn't in the sentence. It's in the coming. (Matthew 11:28)

**GOD:** Tonight you came. That's the first step most people never take.

`[TRUTH CARD]` **"Rest isn't found in reading the verse. It's found in coming to Him."**

---

## 2:50 – THE "WHAT IF" STONES

**ME:** But my mind won't switch off. What if I lose my job? What if I'm thirty and still figuring it out? What if everyone moves on without me?

**GOD:** Can worrying add a single hour to your life? (Matthew 6:27)

**ME:** No. But it feels responsible.

**GOD:** It isn't responsibility. It's carrying tomorrow with today's strength.

**GOD:** Look at the birds outside your window. They don't plan five years ahead. I feed them anyway. (Matthew 6:26)

**GOD:** Don't worry about tomorrow. Tomorrow will worry about itself. Each day has enough trouble of its own. (Matthew 6:34)

`[SCREEN]` Two stones lift out of the backpack: `What if I fail?` and `Tomorrow`. They're set down gently.
`[SFX]` Two soft *thunks*.

**GOD:** Give Me those two. You were never meant to carry tomorrow today.

`[TRUTH CARD]` **"Worry is carrying tomorrow with today's strength."**

`[TODAY'S ASSIGNMENT]` 📝 *Write tonight's worries on paper. Next to each one, write: "Is this mine to carry today?" If not, pray it, and leave it on the page.*

## 4:00 – SEEK FIRST

**ME:** So what do I do? Quit everything?

**GOD:** No. Reorder everything.

**GOD:** Scroll to the bottom of your to-do list.

`[SCREEN]` Her laptop list scrolls down to item 23: *"Pray (if there's time)"*

⏸

**ME:** …oh.

**GOD:** You've been seeking everything else first and fitting Me in at the end.

**GOD:** Seek first the kingdom of God and His righteousness, and all these things will be added to you. (Matthew 6:33)

**GOD:** Added. Not taken away. When I'm first, the rest finds its place.

`[SCREEN]` Item 23 lifts off the bottom of the list and slides up to the very top, glowing gold: **1. Pray.**
`[SCREEN]` Another stone lifts out of the backpack: `5-year plan`. *Thunk.*

**GOD:** "First" doesn't mean "also." It means first.

`[TRUTH CARD]` **"Seek first means first, not fitted in."**

`[TODAY'S ASSIGNMENT]` 📝 *Tomorrow, before your phone, before your email, before your list: give God the first ten minutes. Ask Him what matters most today.*

---

## 5:20 – PATTERN BREAK: THE CALENDAR

`[SCREEN]` A calendar notification slides down: *"Tomorrow: 6:30 AM choir rehearsal · 9:00 AM work · 1:00 PM fundraiser call · 6:00 PM babysit for Kemi · 8:00 PM help Tunde move · 10:00 PM side hustle"*
`[SFX]` Six reminder chimes, one after another, faster and faster.

**ME:** I laughed. I don't know why I laughed. I think I'm going to cry.

**GOD:** Who said yes to all of these?

**ME:** …me.

**GOD:** Did I ask you to?

⏸

**ME:** I just didn't want to let anyone down.

**GOD:** Even I said no. Crowds were looking for Me, and I often slipped away to quiet places to pray. (Luke 5:16)

**GOD:** If I needed to withdraw, you're allowed to as well.

`[SCREEN]` The stone `Everyone's yes` lifts out. *Thunk.*

`[TRUTH CARD]` **"Every yes to everyone is a no to rest."**

`[TODAY'S ASSIGNMENT]` 📝 *Cancel or hand off ONE thing on this week's calendar. Kindly. Without a long apology.*

---

## 6:20 – THE TWIST: WHAT A YOKE REALLY IS

`[MUSIC]` Fades almost to nothing.

**ME:** Can I be honest? I don't get the other part of that verse.

**ME:** "My yoke is easy and My burden is light." Following You doesn't feel easy. It feels like one more thing on the list.

**GOD:** Do you know what a yoke is?

**ME:** Some kind of farm thing?

**GOD:** 😄 Yes. A farm thing.

**GOD:** It's a wooden beam that joins two oxen together, so they pull side by side.

**GOD:** When a farmer trained a young ox, he would yoke it next to an older, stronger one. The young one walked beside it and learned. The strong one carried the weight.

⏸
`[SCREEN]` Behind the chat, a simple warm illustration fades in: two oxen under one wooden yoke, a small one and a large one, walking together across a golden field.

**GOD:** A yoke is built for two.

**GOD:** You've been wearing a yoke made for two, and pulling it alone.

`[MUSIC]` The piano returns, warm and full.

**GOD:** My yoke is easy not because the work is small. It's easy because I'm in it with you, and I'm the one carrying the weight.

**GOD:** "Take My yoke upon you and learn from Me, for I am gentle and humble in heart, and you will find rest for your souls." (Matthew 11:29–30)

**GOD:** Learn from Me means walk at My pace. You've been running. I walk.

`[TRUTH CARD]` **"A yoke is built for two. You were never meant to pull alone."**

---

## 7:40 – THE CLOSE

`[SCREEN]` The backpack, now almost empty. Only two small stones are left: `Tomorrow's work` and `Tolu's birthday`.

**GOD:** Look in the bag now.

**ME:** It's almost empty. Just tomorrow's work. And Tolu's birthday.

**GOD:** Those are good things. Carry them with Me, not for Me.

**ME:** I think I can actually breathe.

**GOD:** Then close the laptop. Go to sleep.

**GOD:** Seek Me first in the morning. I'll be there before your alarm.

**ME:** You're always up first.

**GOD:** Always.

`[SCREEN]` The laptop closes. The room goes dark. The phone screen dims. On the floor by the bed, the backpack sits, light and nearly flat.
`[SFX]` A soft *ding*. "Seen."

*(VO)* What's one stone you're putting down tonight? Write it in the comments. Just one word. Then actually leave it there.

*(VO)* Subscribe for the next chat, because next time I'm asking the question I've been too ashamed to ask.

`[SCREEN]` End card: *"Next chat: 'God, am I too far gone?'"*

## PRODUCTION NOTES

### The backpack
- Keep it small and simple, in the bottom corner, so it never competes with the chat. A soft-shaded illustration works better than a realistic one.
- Each stone's label must be readable on a phone. Use 3–4 words at most.
- Every stone removed gets the same soft *thunk*. By the end, viewers will be waiting for that sound.
- Final shot: the nearly empty backpack on the floor. It's the episode's thumbnail moment.

### The yoke illustration (6:20)
- This is the heart of the episode. Show two oxen under a wooden yoke, side by side, one smaller and one larger, walking through a warm golden field. Slow, peaceful, no rush.
- If you use AI video, prompt: *"Two oxen yoked together under one wooden beam, a young small ox beside a large strong one, walking slowly through a golden wheat field at sunset, warm light, peaceful, cinematic, photoreal."*

### Delivery
- She sounds frayed, not dramatic. The cold open is the voice of someone who has been "fine" for too long.
- "I laughed. I don't know why I laughed. I think I'm going to cry." Let both happen in the read.
- God's voice is the calmest in the series here. Every line unhurried. "I walk." should sound like a smile.

### Why it holds attention
1. **Instant recognition:** a 23-item to-do list at 1 AM.
2. **A strong reframe in the first 30 seconds:** "You're carrying things I never handed you."
3. **A visual payoff that builds:** the backpack empties stone by stone, and viewers stay to see it empty.
4. **The calendar break:** six chimes in a row, funny and painful at once.
5. **A reveal about a familiar verse:** most viewers have never heard what a yoke actually was.
6. **An easy, personal comment prompt:** "One word you're putting down tonight."

### Shorts (5 clips)
- "Have you ever actually come? Or have you just read the sign?" (1:50)
- "Worry is carrying tomorrow with today's strength." (2:50)
- Item 23: "Pray (if there's time)" → "…oh." (4:00, the strongest clip)
- The calendar: six chimes → "Who said yes to all of these?" (5:20)
- "Do you know what a yoke is?" → "A yoke is built for two." (6:20)
