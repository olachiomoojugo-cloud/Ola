# "Texting God: How Do I Actually Live My Purpose?"

**Format:** Animated chat between a young person (the narrator) and God
**Narrator:** A young man or young woman, early-to-mid 20s. They read their own messages *and* read God's replies out loud as they arrive.
**Topic:** Practical ways to live in my purpose
**Runtime:** about 9–10 minutes (about 1,400 spoken words)

---

## HOW TO READ THIS SCRIPT

| Tag | What it means |
|---|---|
| **ME:** | A message the narrator types. Read it like you're talking to yourself: honest, a little tired, real. |
| **GOD:** | A message from God. The narrator reads it slower and softer, as if reading something that stopped them in their tracks. |
| *(VO)* | The narrator speaking to the viewer, not typing. Talk straight into the camera mic. |
| `[SCREEN]` | What the animation shows. |
| `[SFX]` / `[MUSIC]` | Sound cues. |
| ⏸ | A beat of silence. **Keep it.** Silence is what keeps people watching. |

**Chat look (keep it the same in every episode so the series is recognisable):**
- Dark-mode chat screen. Time in the corner reads **2:47 AM**.
- **ME** bubbles: plain grey, right side.
- **GOD** bubbles: warm gold with a soft glow, left side. The contact name is **"Father"**, with a small light where the profile photo would be. Status under the name: *"Always online."*
- The typing indicator ("• • •") shows before **every** God reply. Hold it a little longer before the big lines.
- When a key truth lands, pull it out of the bubble and show it full-screen in bold type (marked `[TRUTH CARD]`).
- Each of the 7 steps ends with a `[TODAY'S ASSIGNMENT]` sticky note that slides in. Viewers will screenshot these.

`[SCREEN]` "Delivered." Nothing. Then "• • •"

**GOD:** I've been waiting for you to ask the real question.

`[SCREEN]` The narrator's cursor blinks. They type… delete… type again.

**ME:** What's my purpose? Like actually. Because I'm 24 and I feel like I'm wasting my whole life.

⏸
`[SCREEN]` "• • •" holds for 3 full seconds.

**GOD:** You're not wasting it.
**GOD:** You're waiting on it. And those are two very different things.

`[MUSIC]` A soft piano starts.
`[SCREEN]` Title slams in: **"I ASKED GOD HOW TO LIVE MY PURPOSE"**

*(VO)* If you've ever felt like everyone else got a map and you got… nothing, stay with me. What He told me next changed how I spend every single day. And the last thing He said? I wasn't ready for it.

**GOD:** First question. Who are you?

⏸

## PRODUCTION NOTES

### Narrator delivery
- **Your own messages (ME):** natural speed, a bit casual. Laugh a little at the emoji lines ("…my phone 😬"). This is what makes people feel like *you're* them.
- **God's messages:** about 20% slower. Lower your voice slightly. Never sound preachy. Read them like you're reading a message that made you sit up in bed.
- **(VO) lines:** straight to the viewer, like a voice note to a friend.
- **Don't fill the ⏸ beats.** Two seconds of silence with a typing indicator on screen is the strongest attention tool in this whole format.

### Why this keeps people watching
1. **An open loop in the first 30 seconds.** "The last thing He said, I wasn't ready for." Viewers stay to find out.
2. **"The 7th one nobody does."** A second open loop that pulls them all the way through the list.
3. **The 1/7 counter.** People can always see how far along they are, and they hate leaving a list unfinished.
4. **A pattern break at about 5:20.** The Instagram notification resets attention right where it usually drops, and it ties straight into Step 5.
5. **The typing indicator.** Each "• • •" is a small cliffhanger. Make the hold longer before the big lines.
6. **Truth cards and assignment stickies.** Built to be screenshotted, shared, and clipped into Shorts.
7. **The emotional twist at 8:20.** "What if it's too late?" is the question many viewers are quietly asking. Answering it there makes the ending stick.
8. **An end card that teases the next chat,** so they click the next video.

### Turning it into Shorts (7 ready-made clips)
Every step is a Short on its own, 45–60 seconds. Open each one on the strongest line:
- "What is that in your hand?" → "…my phone?" (Step 2)
- "Your pain is not your disqualification. It's your direction." (Step 3)
- "I don't give floodlights. I give lamps." (Step 7)
- "It's late. But it's not over." (The Twist)
