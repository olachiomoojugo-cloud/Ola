# Google Flow Prompt Pack: "Texting God: How Do I Actually Live My Purpose?"

Ready-to-paste prompts for **Google Flow** (labs.google/flow, which runs on Google's Veo video model). These are the 10 priority shots, plus 2 swaps for the shots ManyMotions already made.

---

## SETUP (do this once)

1. Open **labs.google/flow**, sign in with the Google account that has your AI plan, and create a project called **"Texting God – Purpose"**.
2. Set the aspect ratio to **Landscape (16:9)**.
3. Pick the model. **Veo Fast** costs fewer credits and is fine for most shots. Use the full **Veo** (Quality) model for the emotional close-ups: shots 4 (tear) and 10 (phone down).
4. **Upload your narrator as an ingredient.** This keeps her the same woman in every shot.
   - Download the portrait from ManyMotions (open it while signed in, then save it):
     https://manymotions.com/api/media-forge/asset/a06458a5-61b9-4c60-af4a-517f69bbad36/image-0.png
   - In Flow, add it as an **image / ingredient** and name it **Ola** (or any name you like). Use that same name in the prompts below.
   - No portrait? Generate one in Flow first with the **CHARACTER** text below, then save the best result as your ingredient.

**CHARACTER (use it if you have no portrait):**
> A young Black woman around 23, natural shoulder-length coily hair loosely tied up, warm brown skin, oversized faded grey hoodie, tired but thoughtful eyes.

### Flow-specific tips
- **Veo adds sound to every clip.** Each prompt ends with *"Ambient sound only, no dialogue, no music"* so you get clean room tone. You'll add your voiceover and music in the editor.
- Clips are about **8 seconds**. That's longer than you need, so trim in your editor.
- **Shots with her face:** use **Ingredients to Video** and select your Ola ingredient.
- **Shots without her face:** use **Text to Video**.
- If a shot comes out with garbled text on a screen, regenerate it. Don't fight it; the "no text" line usually fixes it.
- **Download every clip in 1080p** (choose the upscaled download) before you edit.

## ALL OTHER SHOTS

Every prompt in `practical-ways-to-live-in-my-purpose-video-prompts.md` works in Flow. To adapt one:
1. Replace `[CHARACTER]` with **Ola** and use Ingredients to Video, or paste the CHARACTER text and use Text to Video.
2. Replace `[STYLE]` with: *"Cinematic, moody, deep navy and charcoal shadows, warm gold light as the only accent, shallow depth of field, soft film grain, 35mm film look, photoreal. No text."*
3. Add: *"Ambient sound only, no dialogue, no music."*

**Never show God as a person.** Keep God as light: a glow, a sunrise beam, a lamp.
